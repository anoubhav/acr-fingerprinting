# Rights and licenses

## Audio files

SD-RR is an aggregate of separately licensed musical works. There is no single
license covering every file in `references/` or `queries/`.

For each `reference_id`, `attribution.csv` records:

- the work title and creator;
- the original Jamendo source URL;
- the exact license name and URL supplied with Song Describer;
- whether the license includes NonCommercial or ShareAlike conditions.

The files in `references/` are redistributed source recordings. Each file in
`queries/` is a ten-second excerpt that was loudness-normalized and acoustically
re-recorded; it must be handled as an adaptation under the corresponding
track-level license. Preserve the title, creator, source, license link, and
modification notice when redistributing either form.

The retained set contains 287 CC BY-NC-SA works, 150 CC BY-SA works, 37 CC BY
works, 14 CC BY-NC works, and 8 Free Art License works. NonCommercial files may
not be used for commercial purposes. ShareAlike adaptations must be distributed
under the license required by the original work. Consult the exact URL in
`attribution.csv`; do not silently replace an older or jurisdiction-ported
license with a newer license version.
