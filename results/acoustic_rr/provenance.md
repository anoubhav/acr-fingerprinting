# SD-RR real acoustic transfer evidence

The official SD-RR v1.0.0 release is DOI [10.5281/zenodo.22169646](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22169646), created by Jiheng Li and released 2026-08-30. The accompanying [POLARIS repository](https://github.com/JihengLi/POLARIS) was inspected at revision `fe8943897d3c723060d31485d83d042c9d6a3057`; its pinned downloader supplies independent expected SHA256 values. The release's authoritative files are `DATASET_CARD.md`, `manifest.csv`, `reference_manifest.csv`, `attribution.csv`, `LICENSES.md`, `dataset_config.json` and `dataset_summary.json`. These are preserved under `data/`; `acquisition_audit.json` records digest and archive checks.

The release has 496 Song Describer/Jamendo MP3 references and 1,488 mono PCM16/44.1kHz ten-second phone recordings: three non-overlapping excerpts per reference. References retain the source corpus's approximately two-minute encodings. The original 706-source pool excludes 209 NoDerivatives works and one short source. The query excerpts were levelled toward -20dBFS RMS with a -1dBFS peak ceiling before playback. A 2023 14-inch MacBook Pro at 40% system volume played through its built-in speakers; an iPhone 14 Pro Max about 1.6 m away captured Apple Voice Memos recordings. The publisher reports 460 sources in an apartment and 36 outdoors, but explicitly states that no per-query room/environment mapping exists.

Four capture-session labels occur: `batch-037-706` (393 sources/1,179 queries), `batch-384-473-rerecord` (59/177), `batch-001-036` (27/81), and `batch-extra-17-rerecord` (17/51). Every source's three excerpts share one session. Ground-truth offsets combine selected reference positions with synchronization-chirp alignment at the batch beginning; playback clock scale is fixed at1.0 rather than separately fitted per query. The released table has no alignment_warning values. It contains 243 queries whose independent upstream spectral QC expected-rank is not 1. We retain every query and expose QC strata; no filtering is chosen from our method's results. The accompanying paper reports agreement of separate spectral alignment with chirp offsets within 0.1 s for 99.1% of queries; that is a publisher statement, not a new verification here.

Both archives downloaded directly through the official Zenodo API. Sizes are 2,346,873,899B references and1,060,140,686B queries. SHA256 values are `e3c33f1ab71080dfbac5f850324888fd4edba3d3de55cb223da8713233d7efdb` and `48c27ce1dbc0244d8461dd8ab17256d17afb3345041614c7f94a2e527776dbb5`. Zenodo MD5 checks and all 1,984 individual-media SHA256 checks passed. Archive safety rejects traversal, symlinks, encryption, duplicate names and unexpected top-level directories. Audio remains in `work/` and is not redistributed.

The native protocol indexes all 496 references and evaluates all 1,488 queries. It is closed-set: it provides neither native OOV examples nor native rejection calibration. We therefore report source-candidate correctness and source-plus-position correctness at native 0.1 s, with 2 s as a separately labelled alignment sensitivity. The original FMA-fitted PCA32 and frontend/matcher are frozen. SD-RR provides no fit sources; no PCA or representation selection is performed on its native test queries. A centered 5 s view, if run, is an adaptation of the ten-second recording and adjusts the reference offset by 2.5 s; it is not the original native query task.

A second protocol is explicitly an adapted open-set/capture-session transfer test. The largest session supplies calibration, while all three remaining sessions are held out. Known-versus-unknown enrollment is fixed by normalized creator-group hash before scoring: SHA256 of `sdrr-acoustic-open-v1` plus a NUL plus NFKC-normalized/trimmed/casefolded creator, first 64 bits below2^63 means known. All sources of an unknown creator remain absent from the gallery. Counts are 184 calibration-known sources/552 queries;209 calibration-unknown / 627;47 held-out known/141;56 held-out unknown/168. The gallery has 231 sources. All three clips of a source remain within a single calibration/test scope; unknown creators do not occur in the gallery. Fifty-one creator strings are shared between calibration and test scopes and must not be presented as creator-disjoint calibration. Gates use only the 627 real-acoustic calibration negatives at a declared 1% empirical target. Held-out false accepts must be reported with source/creator grouping and the limited capture-session counts; no rare-FPR, room or device-population guarantee follows.

Source exposure is audited by byte digests and normalized creator/title matches, not by comparing incompatible Jamendo and FMA numeric IDs. No encoded reference SHA256 or exact normalized creator/title pair overlaps the existing 1,052 PEX reference entries. This narrow absence does not prove composition disjointness, especially because different excerpts or encodings can describe the same recording. A broader FMA metadata audit will be retained when complete. The NMFP authors' dataset-creation documentation says all music training sources are FMA and describes the FMA-small/medium development superset; a different corpus name alone still does not establish universal training/composition non-overlap.

Reference durations range 33.891375–119.994625s, all declared source intervals are contained, and no annotation clamp is needed. Final current protocol hashes are native `64754a1f1374630c010a3e6e96465e3c09247382fe6b79c70bc818b5cbbc261a` and session adaptation `cf6644dff657a84a4d1aabd632c0ec5a7f538c4bf2b79d34a013901e3ca8ded8`; the only compatibility correction from initial files added explicit gallery_roles without changing source/crop/model data.

Broader FMAmetadata audit checks 106,574 tracks and finds 10 SD-RR exact normalized creator/title matches (11 FMA IDs); five SD-RR sources fall in FMA-small/medium, the documented possibleNMFP training superset. Sensitivities exclude either all 30 queries of those 10 sources or 15 queries of the five potential training sources, without altering gallery/gates. Source220/FMA043613 also appeared among prior additional calibration negatives; source 151 / FMA 023037 among prior additional test negatives. Original 94-source PCA fit and earlier PEX reference entries have no exact title/creator or encoded-byte matches. These are conservative metadata flags rather than proof of identical encodings or a complete alias/composition audit. `acoustic_rr_data.py overlap` reproduces the audit.

Native public MinHash has nonempty 127/50 descriptors on every 10/5 s phone query and no unsupported inputs, but returns no candidate for 1432/1461 out of 1488 respectively. The same unmodified 4-vote pipeline identifies 20/20 fixed-position clean-source controls at10s and5s, in the same 496-source gallery. These controls choose first 20 metadata reference IDs and fixed 10 s source starts; they are outside the phone benchmark and do not score or tune its ground-truth frames. Primary phone predictions are unchanged.

Unknown uncertainty: the adapted held-out pool has 56 source recordings and 168 correlated clips from three capture sessions.
